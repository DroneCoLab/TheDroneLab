The Drone Lab

Teaching software engineering in the age of AI, with drones.

Coming soon

Join our hands-on tutorial at ICSE 2027, on the Tuesday of ICSE week.

The Drone Lab is a lightweight, multi-UAV software engineering environment that runs on an ordinary laptop. No drones and no prior UAV experience are needed.

Software engineering with AI

AI coding agents let students build larger, integrated systems while staying responsible for requirements, architecture, integration and verification.

Software engineering for Physical AI

Autonomous systems that perceive and act in the physical world bring uncertainty, safety and human-oversight challenges that conventional assignments rarely touch.

Simulation to reality

The same application software that runs in simulation can fly physical drones.

Use AI, but verify, challenge, understand, and take responsibility for the engineered system.

The platform, teaching materials and course structure are being prepared for an open-source release.
